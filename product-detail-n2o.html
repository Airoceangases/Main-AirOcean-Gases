About This Gas

Nitrous
Oxide

Nitrous oxide is a colorless, non-flammable oxidizing gas with a faintly sweet odor. It is one of the most broadly applied specialty gases in North America, spanning medical care, semiconductor manufacturing, food processing, and analytical chemistry. As a liquefied gas stored under its own vapor pressure (~750 psi at 70 °F), it offers exceptional volumetric yield per cylinder — making it cost-effective at scale while maintaining the high purity levels demanded by critical processes.

In healthcare, nitrous oxide is an established analgesic and anesthetic adjunct used in procedural sedation, dental care, and labor analgesia. Medical-grade N₂O (USP/NF) must meet strict pharmacopoeial purity specifications, with total impurities controlled at the sub-ppm level. Airocean Gases supplies medical-grade nitrous oxide in dedicated DOT-compliant cylinders with full chain-of-custody documentation, supporting ambulatory surgical centers, dental clinics, and hospital anesthesia departments throughout the region.

In semiconductor fabrication, N₂O is a primary oxidant source for thermal and plasma-enhanced CVD of silicon dioxide and silicon oxynitride gate dielectric films. Its controlled oxidizing potential enables precise stoichiometry tuning in thin-film deposition — critical for sub-10 nm node transistor gate stacks. Electronic-grade N₂O (99.9995% min.) is also used in III-V compound semiconductor epitaxy and MOCVD processes. Airocean Gases maintains dedicated semiconductor-grade inventory with lot-level certificates of analysis and moisture content verified at the ppb level.
