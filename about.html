Company History

Four Decades of Progress

Founding

Founding of Hongyuan Gas

Hongyuan Gas Technology Co., Ltd. is established in Luoyang, Henan Province, China, with a mission to supply high-quality industrial gases to local manufacturing and medical sectors. A family commitment to precision and reliability is built into the company from day one.

1982

Expansion

Expanding Capabilities

Hongyuan invests in advanced cryogenic separation units, dramatically increasing production capacity for oxygen, nitrogen, and argon to meet China's rapidly growing industrial demand. Infrastructure and safety protocols are scaled to match.

1990s

Specialty Gases

Entering Specialty Gases

Introduction of high-purity Helium, Neon, Krypton, and Xenon production lines — supplying China's rapidly advancing electronics, semiconductor, and research sectors. This marks the beginning of Hongyuan's rare gas specialization.

2005

Certification

Technology & Certification Milestones

Facilities upgraded with automated filling lines. GMP certification obtained and a full ISO Quality Management System implemented — ensuring global-standard quality, traceability, and safety across every product batch.

2015–2020

North America

Airocean Gases Launch

Airocean Gases, LLC is established in the United States to serve the North American market — bringing Hongyuan's 40+ years of rare gas expertise overseas. Headquartered in Delaware, we bridge proven production capability with U.S. customers' exacting standards.

2024

Industry Recognition

GAWDA Membership

Airocean Gases becomes an official member of the Gases and Welding Distributors Association (GAWDA) — joining a network of the most trusted gas distributors in North America. This milestone affirms our commitment to industry standards, safety, and long-term customer partnerships.

2025

Commitment

Health & Safety

We are committed to providing a safe and healthy working environment for employees, customers, and visitors. We have established a comprehensive health and safety management system ensuring all business activities meet the highest safety standards.

Team Support
